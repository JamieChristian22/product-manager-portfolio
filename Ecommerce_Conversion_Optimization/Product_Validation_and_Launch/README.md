# Ecommerce Conversion Optimization — Product Validation & Launch

**Status:** Educational simulation / proposed delivery plan, **not a live product launch**.

**Feature:** Product listing discovery refresh.

**Core problem:** Shoppers struggle to filter and compare products before viewing product details.

**Primary persona:** first-time mobile shoppers.

**Hypothesis:** Clearer filters, comparison cues and product-card information will improve product discovery and purchase conversion.
